About

Hi, I’m Daniel!

I am a 5th year Computer Science undergraduate at Rochester Institute of Technology and I’m currently in my final semester of classes! In my free time, I enjoy exercise, video games, and problem solving. I’m always open to learning new technologies and improving my critical thinking skills. I’m currently looking for full-time employment as a backend or fullstack software engineer and will be available to work starting in January.

This website is still a WIP, as I was prioritizing work while on co-op for the past year. In the future, I plan to add Projects and Blog pages to document both professional and personal experiences.

Over the past year, I was given the opportunity to work at the Vanguard Group as an Application Engineer Intern. I was put directly on a high-functioning Agile team that develops and maintains backend systems that support critical business functions, such as creating an account. I’d independently spent time learning about AWS, but working here gave me the opportunity to apply my skills to solve real issues for clients. I wrote new glue/batch jobs that automatically run at set schedules (daily or weekly depending on purpose), which help maintain data quality across mainframe to AWS Aurora. I enhanced several APIs that used Spring Boot and GraphQL, including fixing bugs, adding new features, and endpoints, as well as ensuring dependencies were up-to-date. I worked directly with our consumers to ensure any enhancements met their use-cases and did thorough end-to-end testing and performance testing to ensure high quality code. I’m looking forward to new challenges and learnings as I start my professional career!
